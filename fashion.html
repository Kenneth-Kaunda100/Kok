Over the past ancient years, African traditional attires defined African roots and culture, it is of no doubt that the evolution of African fashion embedded in ancient African lifestyles has rapidly evolved over the time competing with the global contemporary fashion wears from all across the global dynamics. The transition of ancient African wears and self-decorations profoundly indicate that fashion in its creative was a lifestyle among the African people and this immensely portrays evidence that perhaps Africa is the birth place of fashion since multiple of contemporary fashion today obtains an element and a taste of African artistry. Fashion in Africa in majority opinion backed with numerous evidence, is a lifestyle that has continuously influenced the modern fashion industry from Africa to Paris, across London and various states in the United States on multiple runways.
